# Bounded native archetype observations

Optional project-owned `design/playtests.json` predeclares scripted combat trials for the [isolated native lab](NATIVE-TESTING.md). These exercise cards together under normal combat resources. They are observations of a fixed policy, not campaign playthroughs, win-rate estimates, optimal play, fun scores or human balance approval.

Use [playtests.schema.json](playtests.schema.json). Each scenario fixes an ID, seed, generated-card deck with explicit upgrade flags, `NibbitsNormal` encounter, Ascension 0, `priority-lowest-hp` policy, and turn/play caps. Every distinct deck card must appear exactly once in `priority`; repeated deck copies are allowed. The initial supported encounter/policy are deliberately narrow. Bespoke extension cards, arbitrary encounters, online co-op and campaign routing need separate fixtures.

A plan allows up to six scenarios, 30 deck cards per scenario, eight turns and 60 successful plays per scenario. Total requested budgets cannot exceed 24 turns or 180 plays. The lab's existing one-shot 30–600 second process deadline also applies. Empty/omitted scenarios mean no extra trials; disabled custom cards require an empty/omitted plan. Changes invalidate native/build evidence through the normal input fingerprint.

Run `validate`, `generate`, `prepare-lab`, one-shot `test-native`, then `balance` using the project's pinned CLI. The combined full-pool/six-scenario example used `test-native --lab-id <prepared-id> --timeout 600`; the timeout covers the correctness suite and all observations together. Preparation embeds the validated plan in the test request. Normal character DLLs and staged packages contain no runner or plan. No separate live-game controller or regular save is used.

Each trial starts a fresh run at the character's configured HP and starting relics, with exactly the declared deck/upgrades. It enters a normal native Nibbits encounter without the individual-card fixture's inflated enemy HP, cleared powers, extra Energy or draw fillers. The probe does not play potions or choose draft rewards. Native draws, enemies, turn transitions and character/item effects remain active.

During a player turn the runner attempts cards in declared priority order, retaining native hand order for ties. Single-target cards select the living foe with lowest current HP, with native enemy order breaking ties. Rejected/unaffordable cards are skipped; after a successful play the policy restarts at its first priority, allowing native draw chains. If nothing can be played, it ends the turn and waits for the native enemy response. A turn cap stops after the final permitted turn's enemy response and next draw; a play cap stops immediately after its last permitted successful play. Victory or defeat stops either policy sooner. A timeout/error fails the lab and must not be replayed.

Receipts include the exact scenario, actual initial deck/upgrades, starting relics, resource snapshots, successful card/target/upgrade decisions, per-turn play counts, and final HP/outcome. `victory`, `defeat`, `turn-cap` and `play-cap` are valid recorded outcomes. A successful observation means the bounded protocol ran. Evidence validation rejects missing/duplicate scenarios, altered inputs/decks, missing numeric observations, cap violations, inconsistent counts and outcomes inconsistent with final HP. `balance` exposes current observations separately from its heuristic warnings and reports stale/unperformed trials explicitly.

[Steady Path's plan](examples/steady-path/playtests.json) includes three proposed archetype decks and three controls: immediate play cap, one-turn defense cap, and a no-offense deck. The plan is capped at 22 turns and 89 successful plays in total. Greedy priorities can waste Energy, overfill a hand, or favor defense over a lethal attack. Do not tune cards merely to improve this policy's results. Review decisions and costs against native cards, then collect predeclared campaign/human observations before changing balance. Keep failed/capped runs; do not search seeds until a desired result appears.

The [recorded native trial](../docs/archetype-native-fixture.json) retained all three archetype cap outcomes and the no-offense defeat. A separate small positive-control plan exercised victory and a following fresh-run reset. That intentionally contrived upgraded-AoE deck tests runner branches, not archetype quality. The receipt includes both plans, real resource/decision traces and distinct fixture identities; it is a historical record, not a fresh local pass.
